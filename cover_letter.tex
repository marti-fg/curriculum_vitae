A prototype is the only review that matters for a vehicle controller. Motorsport gave me that review on a short cycle: a nonlinear model, a real-time implementation, and a lap that confirms or kills the law before the day is over. That is the work I want to do at \company: guidance, estimation and control that have to stand up on the vehicle, not only in the model.

At CUPRA Racing I built the multi-physics plant of the TCR hybrid and the Raval 4WD prototype in MATLAB and Simulink: nonlinear tyres, suspension, and a hybrid powertrain with the engine and the electric motor in one model. On that plant I wrote the closed-loop yaw controller that allocates drive and brake torque, and I calibrated it on the car against the measured yaw response. The bachelor's thesis that defines the allocation was graded 100\% and nominated for Honours Distinction. The Cranfield MSc in Autonomous Vehicle Dynamics and Control, which I started in September 2026, is the estimation and robustness layer on the same problem: sensor fusion for GNSS/INS integration, including extended and unscented Kalman filters, guidance and navigation, and classical, state-space and robust control.

The controller was not finished in simulation. I refactored the Vehicle Control Unit of the TCR hybrid in Simulink so the law ran as scheduled embedded software: execution order, task rates, and the real-time path through the gateway VCU on the vehicle CAN bus. I validated that refactor in track testing. I also sized the electric powertrain cooling system and correlated the thermal model to the logged sensors. The session data came from the stack I configured for those tests, MoTeC for acquisition and WinTAX4 for the analysis, following the OptimumG data-driven performance seminar. UPC ecoRacing was the same loop on a Formula Student car with four independent in-wheel motors: the torque-vectoring law, the test sessions I led, and strain-gauge measurements to check that the loads in the model were the loads in the part.

I am a Spanish national. The Cranfield degree runs until August 2027, in parallel with the UPC MSc in Aeronautical Engineering (88.3\% on the 60 ECTS completed). I am available to start in \availability. The conversation worth having is a technical one: the yaw allocation, the VCU task schedule, and the sessions that confirmed both. I can bring the model structure and the track data.
